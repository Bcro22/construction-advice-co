About

Real Construction Experience. Independent Advice.

The Construction Advice Co. exists for property owners who want an experienced construction professional to help them understand what they’re being told before they make an expensive decision.

Built From Real-World Experience

My background spans more than 20 years of residential and commercial construction—from small renovations and single-family homes to large custom residences, restaurants, hotels, commercial spaces, and complex attraction work.

I’ve worked across pre-construction, estimating, field operations, contractor coordination, project management, problem solving, and closeout. That range matters because construction problems rarely fit neatly into one box.

What This Business Is—and Isn’t

  • Independent construction guidance for the owner
  • A second set of experienced eyes on plans, bids, changes, and problems
  • Plain-English answers without a sales pitch
  • Not a replacement for your architect, engineer, attorney, inspector, or contractor
  • Not a construction bid or solicitation to perform your project
