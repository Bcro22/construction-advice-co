FAQ

Questions Before You Ask the Questions.

Here’s how the service works, what you can send, and where the limits are.

What can I send you?

Plans, contractor proposals, estimates, scopes, change orders, invoices, inspection items, photographs, schedules, and relevant contractor communications. The service description tells you the file/page limits for each package.

Are you my contractor?

No. The Construction Advice Co. provides independent construction consulting. I am not bidding your work or replacing the contractor responsible for performing it.

Is this an inspection or engineering opinion?

No. Site observations and document reviews are advisory and based on the information available. They do not replace required inspections, engineering, architecture, code review, or legal advice.

How fast will I get a response?

Free questions are intended for quick general guidance. Paid reviews are scheduled based on workload and complexity. If you have a hard deadline, tell me before purchasing so I can confirm timing.

Can you help if my project is already in trouble?

Yes. Use the $500 Comprehensive Project Review when delays, payment issues, change orders, workmanship concerns, or contractor communication problems overlap and you need help seeing the whole situation. If it needs an on-site visit or ongoing support, send a custom request.

Do you work outside Florida?

Remote consulting may be available outside Florida for general construction guidance and document review. Local code, licensing, legal, and inspection requirements should always be confirmed by the appropriate local professional.
